Metabolic Risk Prediction Tool

Development and Validation of an AI-Based Metabolic Risk Prediction Tool in Patients Receiving Mood Stabilizers

This research tool uses clinical, anthropometric, drug-related, and laboratory parameters to classify metabolic risk into Low, Moderate, or High categories.

What does it assess?

  • Body measurements and blood pressure
  • Glucose and lipid results
  • Mood stabilizer treatment
  • Selected clinical information

Important notice

Research / academic tool only. This prediction does not replace clinical judgment.
